# Optional life-area menu

Use this menu to help someone recognize what they want to focus on. Present a few relevant choices or the whole compact menu when helpful; a supplied goal can bypass it. People may rename, split, merge, hide, or add areas. An area may be maintained, enjoyed, or deferred without acquiring an improvement goal.

| Starter area | Source basis and adaptation |
|---|---|
| Physical health | SAMHSA physical; Full Focus body |
| Mental and emotional wellbeing | SAMHSA emotional; Full Focus mind |
| Relationships and belonging | SAMHSA social; Full Focus love/family/community |
| Work and contribution | SAMHSA occupational; Designing Your Life work; contribution broadens beyond employment |
| Money | SAMHSA financial; Full Focus money |
| Learning and growth | SAMHSA intellectual; OECD education |
| Home and surroundings | SAMHSA environmental; OECD housing/environment |
| Play and rest | Designing Your Life play; Full Focus hobbies; rest is a product adaptation |
| Meaning and spirituality | SAMHSA spiritual; wording permits secular interpretation |

These nine are a design synthesis, not a validated assessment or universal taxonomy. Offer more specific prompts for partner/intimacy, family/caregiving, friends/community, civic participation, or safety when relevant. Confirm actual roles and preferences rather than assuming them. Store only the user's selected labels and meanings; suggestions are not personal facts. Equal attention or a composite life score is not a requirement.

## Anchors

- [SAMHSA wellness guide](https://store.samhsa.gov/sites/default/files/sma16-4957.pdf): eight dimensions in a wellness/recovery context.
- [Full Focus LifeScore](https://assessments.fullfocus.co/lifescore/): current nine-domain commercial assessment.
- [Designing Your Life workbook](https://designingyour.life/designing-your-life-workbook/): health, work, play, and love dashboard.
- [OECD wellbeing framework](https://www.oecd.org/en/data/tools/well-being-data-monitor.html): broader living conditions; population indicators do not directly prescribe personal goals.

# Review and adapt

Read [working context](../steward-memory/references/working-context.md) and [review and learning](references/review-learning.md). This skill owns interpreting what happened and deciding what to change.

1. Retrieve the relevant period's plan version, intended outcome, available observations, and the user's experience. Separate missing data from nonadherence and completion from benefit. Completion: the review has an honest baseline and no invented progress.
2. Compare outcome, adherence, effort, enjoyment, friction, and competing commitments. Ask whether the goal still matters and whether its strategy serves the parent purpose. Completion: explanations identify plausible causes and uncertainty rather than blaming discipline or treating a proxy as success.
3. Choose keep, change, pause, retire, or investigate. Use [goals](../steward-goals/SKILL.md) for lifecycle changes; [routines](../steward-routines/SKILL.md) or [planning](../steward-plan/SKILL.md) for revisions; [experiments](../steward-experiments/SKILL.md) when a bounded test would resolve uncertainty. Completion: the review changes the operational plan where warranted, rather than only producing commentary.
4. Propagate changed constraints and decisions to connected records and outputs. Use [scheduling](../steward-schedule/SKILL.md) for authorized calendar or follow-up changes. Inspect concrete adjacent improvements via [opportunities](../steward-opportunities/SKILL.md) without turning a focused review into a whole-life audit. Completion: each affected item is updated, pending with a reason, or explicitly unaffected.
5. Save conclusions, supporting observations, plan version, and the next useful review through [memory](../steward-memory/SKILL.md). Completion: a future task can tell what was learned, what changed, and what would trigger reconsideration.

For local Apple Calendar or Reminders access, use [Steward Apple](../steward-apple/SKILL.md).

# Research and quality

For important decisions use current external evidence where it materially improves the result. Start with the decision to resolve, then use suitable primary/authoritative sources, systematic reviews, trials, high-quality observational work and expert synthesis. Inspect applicability, evidence quality, effect size and uncertainty. A guideline may establish a useful floor; an exemplar can reveal better implementation. Neither alone proves the best individual plan.

Keep a compact evidence record: claim/choice, supporting source URL, date checked, relevance, limitations and what is still unknown. Show a short linked reason in the result. Separate independent evidence, practitioner implementation, manufacturer claims and user-reported experience. Verify historical facts before using them as current advice.

## Exemplar checkpoint

Before settling a materially new approach or important deliverable, inspect strong examples of the actual experience or artifact. State the useful mechanism, what transfers to this context, and what should be rejected or adapted. Retain a compact source-to-choice record, then verify the output against those criteria. Reuse current, applicable findings; reopen research when the decision or evidence changes. A familiar routine edit need not restart the search. Examples demonstrate implementation; evidence must separately support consequential factual claims. Research is complete when remaining uncertainty would not materially change the current action, or is explicitly carried into a question or experiment.

## QUALITY WITHOUT DOGMA

For a physical product, first establish whether it serves the goal. Then compare relevant dimensions:

- Efficacy, formulation/material, useful dose or serving, suitability and durability.
- User dietary/allergy/ethical constraints, convenience, taste and existing resources.
- Identity and potency, independent tests and their exact scope, batch transparency, contaminants relevant to the category, ingredient and company transparency.
- Heavy metals, microbes, PFAS, pesticides, residual solvents, additives or packaging exposure only when meaningful to that product/use; distinguish demonstrated risk from unsupported marketing.
- Price, pack size, delivery/tax where available, cost per useful unit, replacement cadence and who pays.

Translate “clean” into concrete criteria. A certification covering prohibited substances does not establish every contaminant claim. Verify the actual product/batch and certificate where available. Unknown testing is unknown, not clean or toxic. Organic, premium pricing, a famous founder and “chemical-free” claims are not automatic wins. Pay more when a material benefit warrants it; find lower-cost ways to achieve comparable quality and reconcile the choice against finances.

## Health and supplements

A health Plan needs evidence for the intervention and suitability for the person. Do not infer a diagnosis or silently prescribe around an uncertain product, dose, interaction or health condition. Verify decision-critical details; help with unaffected logistics while those remain open. Professional evaluation can be a needed step, with a prepared question list or relevant summary when useful.

A supplement review considers necessity, food/fortification alternatives, effective form/dose, dietary constraints, existing intake, interactions/contraindications, quality/testing, contaminants, timing and cost per effective dose. Distinguish an essential gap from an optional performance candidate. Investigate relevant candidates systematically rather than copying a generic nutrient list, and consider removing low-value existing spending. Manufacturer fasting claims do not establish human longevity outcomes. Do not promote mechanistic or animal findings into proven individual benefits.

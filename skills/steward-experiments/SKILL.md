# Test an uncertainty

Read [working context](../steward-memory/references/working-context.md). Read [research quality](../steward-decide/references/research-quality.md) when evidence or safety affects the proposed change.

1. **Recover the test.** Identify its question, the decision its answer would change, baseline, prior attempts, and actual status. For an existing test, retrieve its saved design and observations. Completion: the uncertainty and current phase are known; elapsed calendar time alone is not evidence of participation.
2. **Work in the current phase.** For design, define the test below and prepare execution. For an ongoing test, preserve its design and help with requested execution or logging; record any design change and its effect on interpretation. For interpretation, assess the available observations below. Completion: the requested phase has its usable result; a prepared design remains prepared until execution is confirmed.
3. **Save and propagate.** Use [memory](../steward-memory/SKILL.md) to save the question, design, actual status, sources, available observations, and any decision. Promote supported results into affected plans or routines; mark inconclusive findings. Completion: future work can recover what is known and what remains uncertain, with changed plans reconciled.

## Design

Select a feasible change, duration, comparison where practical, observations, burden limit, and keep/change/remove criteria. Account for obvious confounders and set stop or escalation conditions appropriate to the intervention. A short adherence test can assess fit; it cannot establish a long-term medical effect.

Use [routines](../steward-routines/SKILL.md) for repeated steps and working tracking calculations, [planning](../steward-plan/SKILL.md) for artifacts, and [scheduling](../steward-schedule/SKILL.md) for requested time or reminders. Completion: the user can perform and record a test whose observations inform the stated decision, within existing mode and authorization.

## Interpretation

Compare actual observations against the saved criteria, including missing data, the user's experience, and changes to the design. Distinguish personal fit from proof of a mechanism or universal causality. Completion: a qualified keep/change/remove decision or a specific unresolved question; absent observations remain missing rather than assumed results.

# Find useful opportunities

Read [working context](../steward-memory/references/working-context.md). This skill owns discovering and qualifying possible improvements; it does not create new commitments by itself.

1. Bound the search to the requested area or a concrete observation in current work. Retrieve relevant constraints, prior rejected suggestions, and available resources. Completion: candidates are grounded in the user's situation rather than a generic list of life optimizations.
2. Inspect the few promising candidates. Estimate net benefit after money, time, effort, risk, dependencies, and lost value. For comparisons use [decisions](../steward-decide/SKILL.md), including the user's actual decision horizon. Completion: a candidate has enough evidence to be useful; speculative savings are labeled as estimates.
3. Resolve the smallest missing fact that changes feasibility while continuing independent preparation. Compare doing nothing where relevant. Completion: recommend only worthwhile candidates, or report that the bounded search found none; do not invent an opportunity to fill a quota.
4. Prepare useful next work. Use [administration](../steward-admin/SKILL.md) for execution already authorized, or present the exact consequential action that needs a decision. Completion: a savings idea becomes a calculated comparison or prepared action, while cancellation, contact, spending, and enrollment retain their own authorization boundaries.
5. Save durable preferences, dismissed candidates, or confirmed follow-through via [memory](../steward-memory/SKILL.md). Completion: future suggestions can respect what the user values and avoid repetitive low-value nudges.

During another task, finish its objective first unless the opportunity resolves a dependency. Continuous watching requires a separately authorized and verified scheduler; this skill only runs when invoked.

# Meet Steward

[Get set up](install.md) · **First conversation** · [Everyday use](using-steward.md)

Say **“Help me get started with Steward.”** You don't need to know which skill to choose, have your goals figured out, or prepare a list of everything on your plate. Steward should explain the next step and walk you through it.

## A place to remember

Steward recommends **Obsidian** if you want an app for browsing your personal wiki. The wiki is a folder of readable Markdown notes: useful context, goals, plans, routines, and where you left off. An existing vault works, and its name is yours to choose. A plain folder works too; Obsidian is optional.

Steward creates the linked structure and checks that it can save and retrieve information. It preserves existing notes. If opening the vault or connecting your agent's workspace needs a manual step, it gives you that step. Creating files does not by itself configure Obsidian, sync, or backups.

## A little context, then useful options

Next, choose the apps you want Steward to use. On a supported Mac these can include Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Contacts, Mail, and Messages. Other connections depend on the tools your agent host actually provides. You can choose a few, choose all offered sources, or skip them.

Steward explains the scope, checks access, then reviews upcoming commitments, open tasks, and recent relevant material. It should bring back a few grounded possibilities, such as preparing a reimbursement claim before its deadline or finding room for a routine you wanted to start. It tells you where its picture is incomplete. Installing a plugin alone does not guarantee the current chat can read its data.

If a connection fails, Steward checks usable alternatives and guides the next step. The rest of setup can continue. It should not leave you with an unexplained error or keep asking you to invoke the same unavailable plugin.

## Choose one thing and see it through

You can take one of those suggestions or start with an area of life: health, money, work, relationships, home, or something else. You choose what matters. Steward helps define a useful outcome, checks what it already knows, and asks only for the missing details.

> **You:** I'd like more time to read, but my evenings disappear.
>
> **Steward:** Tuesday looks lighter than the rest of your week. We could start with twenty minutes after dinner. Would that feel manageable?
>
> **You:** Yes. Put it on my personal calendar at eight.
>
> **Steward:** It's on your personal calendar for Tuesday at eight. I've saved the plan so we can adjust it after you've tried it.

That last reply is appropriate only after the calendar action succeeds and is read back. If access is blocked, Steward says what remains unscheduled. You don't have to invoke a planning or calendar skill yourself; Steward handles those handoffs.

## Come back as you are

Say **“What next?”**, **“Here's what happened”**, or bring whatever is on your mind. With the memory folder connected to your workspace, Steward picks up from the saved context. If you chose a trial without storage, bring the continuation note it gives you.

The loop is **Check in → Make progress → Adjust**. You don't need to remember it to use it. Scheduled check-ins happen only when requested and actually configured.

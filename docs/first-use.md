# Your first conversation

[Get set up](install.md) · **First conversation** · [Everyday use](using-steward.md)

Say: **“Help me get started with Steward.”** You can select Steward from the app’s @ picker if you want to be explicit. Ordinary language is enough when the installed skill is available to your agent.

Steward checks what is already ready and follows your answers. It might begin with “What would you like to make a little easier right now?” If you already brought a request, it starts there.

## What you’ll choose

These choices come up as needed, rather than as one long form:

- **Where to remember things.** Reuse a folder or Obsidian vault, create a Markdown folder, or try without saving. Your vault can have any name.
- **Which context helps.** Choose optional Calendar, Reminders, Notes, Contacts, Mail, and Messages connections on a supported Mac. You can skip any of them. Steward checks selected access and explains anything pending.
- **One useful starting point.** A goal, a decision, a message, a crowded week, or an area you want to explore. You do not need a complete list of life goals.

## A conversation might look like this

This is an illustration, not a fixed script or a promise of a particular outcome.

> **You:** I want my evenings to feel less rushed.
>
> **Steward:** What tends to take over your evenings?
>
> **You:** Work runs late, and then I still have chores.
>
> **Steward:** Let’s start with one evening. Is there a small thing you’d like to make room for?
>
> **You:** Twenty minutes to read.
>
> **Steward:** Which evening is usually easiest to protect?

From there, Steward can help make a realistic plan, use selected calendar context if useful, and save the next step. It handles the supporting skills as the conversation changes from choosing a focus to planning. You never need to say “now invoke the planning skill.”

If memory or app choices are still open, Steward fits them into the conversation. If an app is blocked or you prefer to skip it, the plan can continue with the information you provide. A proposal stays a proposal until the relevant app action is authorized and verified.

## Leave with something useful

Your first session can finish with a small plan, a draft, a clarified decision, or another useful result. With memory configured, Steward saves what matters and where to pick up. Without storage, it gives you a continuation note to bring back.

Next time, say **“Here’s what happened”**, **“What next?”**, or bring a new request. You do not restart onboarding or repeat settled choices. [See the everyday loop and optional skill map](using-steward.md).
